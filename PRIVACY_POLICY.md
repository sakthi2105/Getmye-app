# Privacy Policy for GetMye

**Effective Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026

Welcome to **GetMye** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (**GetMye**) and related backend services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By downloading, installing, accessing, or using GetMye, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies or relates to you directly or indirectly:

### A. Personal Information
- **Account Details:** Name, mobile phone number, email address, and delivery addresses provided during registration and profile setup.
- **Order Details:** Information regarding items ordered, delivery instructions, transaction history, and communication with customer support.

### B. Location Information (Foreground & Background Location)
- **Foreground Location:** We collect precise GPS location data when the app is active in the foreground to:
  - Automatically detect your delivery address.
  - Show nearby stores and services.
  - Calculate accurate delivery estimates and fees.
- **Background Location:** If you are a registered delivery partner or when an active order is being delivered and tracked, GetMye may access your location in the background (even when the app is closed or not in use) to enable real-time order tracking, route navigation, and timely delivery updates.

### C. Camera and Media Access
- We may request access to your device’s camera and photo gallery if you choose to upload a profile picture, attach photos for customer support inquiries, or capture delivery confirmation proofs.

### D. Device and Technical Information
- Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, app crash logs, and network connection details.

---

## 2. Payment Information

- We process payments through secure, RBI-authorized third-party payment gateways such as **Cashfree Payments**.
- GetMye **does not store or process your complete credit/debit card numbers, CVV, or net banking passwords** on our servers. All payment processing conforms to PCI-DSS standards maintained by the payment processor.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- **Order Fulfillment:** To process orders, verify deliveries, and facilitate communication between customers, vendors, and delivery partners.
- **Real-Time Tracking:** To provide live location tracking of orders using Google Maps APIs.
- **Push Notifications:** To send order status updates, delivery alerts, OTPs, and important service announcements via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
- **Safety and Fraud Prevention:** To verify user authenticity, prevent fraudulent orders, and enforce our terms of service.
- **Customer Support:** To resolve support tickets, refunds, or delivery queries.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

We may share limited data with trusted third-party providers strictly to perform necessary services on our behalf:
- **Google Maps API:** For address autocompletion, map rendering, and route calculation.
- **Google Firebase:** For push notifications, analytics, and authentication services.
- **Cashfree Payments:** For secure checkout and payment processing.

These third-party service providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they handle user data.

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and physical security measures (including HTTPS/SSL encryption) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

## 6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

- We retain your personal information as long as your account remains active or as needed to comply with legal, tax, or regulatory obligations.
- **Account Deletion:** Users have the right to request the deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time. You can request deletion directly from the app settings or by contacting our support team at **sivasakthi@coderead.in**. Upon verification, your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days, subject to legally required record-keeping.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

GetMye is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18 without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If we discover that a minor has provided us with personal details, we will immediately delete such information.
