Account & Personal Data Deletion

Application: GetMye | Applicable URL: https://getmye.coderead.in/delete-account.html

At GetMye, we respect your right to control your personal data. In full compliance with Google Play’s Account Deletion Policy and global privacy laws, users can permanently delete their GetMye account and all associated personal data at any time, directly through the mobile application or online through this web resource.

Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. You will lose access to order histories, saved addresses, wallet credits/loyalty rewards, and account settings.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

We provide two convenient options to initiate an account and data deletion request:

A Option 1: In-App Self-Serve Deletion (Instant & Recommended)

If you have the GetMye app installed on your smartphone:

  1. Open the GetMye app.
  2. Tap on your Profile / Account icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Go to Settings > Account Security & Privacy.
  4. Tap on "Delete My Account".
  5. Review the data loss confirmation and enter your registered mobile number OTP to verify your identity.
  6. Tap "Confirm Permanent Deletion". Your account will be immediately signed out and scheduled for permanent deletion.

B Option 2: Web / Email Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, you can submit an online request directly using the form below or by emailing our Grievance Officer:

  • Email: sivasakthi@coderead.in
  • Subject: Account and Data Deletion Request - GetMye
  • Required Details: Your registered full name, registered mobile number (+91), and registered email address.

3. Deletion Timeline & Process

  1. Immediate Deactivation (Within 24 Hours): Your account is locked immediately upon receiving the verified request. You will no longer receive marketing communications, push notifications, or order updates.
  2. Primary Database Purge (Within 30 Days): All personal profile data, saved addresses, search history, and live tracking records are permanently deleted from all active servers.
  3. Backup Purge (Up to 90 Days): Encrypted database snapshots and backup archives are overwritten according to our automated 90-day retention cycle.
