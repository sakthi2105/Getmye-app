Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I place an order?
Select your items in the app, confirm your GPS delivery location, choose online payment via Cashfree or Cash on Delivery, and tap "Confirm Order".
2. How do I track my active delivery?
Once a delivery partner accepts your order, you can open live tracking on Google Maps to monitor their location in real-time.
3. What if money was debited but the order failed?
Failed transactions are automatically reversed back to your payment source (bank account or UPI) within 24–48 hours by the Cashfree Payment Gateway.
4. How do I request a cancellation or refund?
Orders can be cancelled before dispatch from the order screen. For dispute resolution or refunds, please reach out directly to +91 9344701545 or sivasakthi@coderead.in.
5. How can I join as a delivery partner?
Open the GetMye app, navigate to Options & Support -> 'Join as Delivery Partner', enter your driving license and vehicle details, and submit for verification.
