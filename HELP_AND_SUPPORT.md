## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Orders & Delivery
**Q1: How do I place an order?**  
- Browse the home screen, select the store or items you want, and add them to your cart.
- Set your delivery location using your live GPS or saved address.
- Choose your payment method (Online via Cashfree or Cash on Delivery) and tap "Confirm Order".

**Q2: How can I track my active order in real-time?**  
- Once a delivery partner accepts your order, you can open the **Live Tracking** page.
- You can view the live GPS movement of your delivery rider, route on Google Maps, and estimated arrival time.

**Q3: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**  
- You can contact your assigned delivery partner directly via the call icon on the order screen or reach out to customer support immediately.

---

### 2. Payments & Cashfree Gateway
**Q4: What payment methods are accepted?**  
- We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
- Online payments are processed safely through the **Cashfree Payment Gateway**.

**Q5: What happens if money was deducted but the order failed?**  
- If your account was debited for an unconfirmed order, Cashfree will automatically process a refund back to your original source account within 24–48 hours (or 3–5 business days depending on your bank).

---

### 3. Cancellations & Refunds
**Q6: How do I cancel an order?**  
- You can cancel your order from the order status screen before the vendor/partner starts processing or dispatching it.
- If the cancellation option is no longer available, call customer support at **+91 9344701545**.

**Q7: When will I receive my refund?**  
- Approved refunds for prepaid orders are credited back to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

---

### 4. Delivery Partners
**Q8: How do I become a GetMye delivery partner?**  
- Open the GetMye app, navigate to **Options & Support**, and select **"Join as Delivery Partner"**.
- Enter your mobile number, submit your vehicle and driving license details for verification, and start receiving delivery requests once approved.

**Q9: Who do delivery partners contact for order issues?**  
- Delivery partners can call support directly at **+91 9344701545** or use the in-app partner helpline for immediate route or merchant assistance.

---

### 5. Account & Privacy
**Q10: How do I update my profile or delete my account?**  
- You can edit your profile details from the **Profile** screen.
- To request complete account and data deletion, email us at **sivasakthi@coderead.in** with your registered phone number.
